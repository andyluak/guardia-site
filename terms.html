Train safely

Boxing training is hard exercise. Talk to a doctor before you start if you have a health condition, are pregnant, or have an injury. Clear the space around you, and stop if you feel pain or dizziness. Guardia is not medical advice.

Counting

Guardia estimates your punches, combos, and guard from the camera. It can miss a punch or count one you did not throw, and its scores are a guide, not a judgment.

Subscriptions

A subscription renews automatically through your Apple account until you cancel it. A free trial turns into a paid subscription at its end unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. Cancel any time in your iPhone Settings, under your Apple Account and Subscriptions.

Guardia estima tus golpes, combos y guardia con la cámara. Puede perder un golpe o contar uno que no lanzaste, y sus puntajes son una guía, no un juicio.
