Guardia

A boxing coach that sees you.

Stand your phone up, step back, and box. Guardia calls the combos out loud, counts and names every punch, and tells you when your guard drops. In English and Spanish.

Coming soon to the App Store

What you get

  • Live combo calls in a coach's voice
  • Every punch counted and named: jab, cross, hooks, and uppercuts
  • A guard check and one fix after each round
  • A 4-week program for beginners, and free rounds you set up yourself
  • A 20-second punch sprint and a 30-second skill test, free to try

Private by design

Your phone does the counting. Guardia never records, saves, or sends video, and your workouts stay on your phone. There is no account to make.
