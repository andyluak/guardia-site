Box at home

A boxing coach that sees you.

Stand your phone up, step back, and box. Guardia calls the combos out loud, counts and names every punch, and tells you when your guard drops.

Coming soon to the App StoreiPhone

On the card

  1. 01

    Combos called live

    Your coach calls each combo out loud, in English or Spanish. The camera checks you threw it.

  2. 02

    Every punch counted

    Jab, cross, hooks, and uppercuts, named as you throw them, round after round.

  3. 03

    One fix a round

    A guard check while you box, and one thing to work on when the bell goes.

  4. 04

    A plan from your first jab

    Four weeks for beginners, free rounds you set up yourself, and a 20-second sprint to beat.

Private by design

Your phone does the counting. Guardia never records, saves, or sends video, your workouts stay on your phone, and there is no account to make.
